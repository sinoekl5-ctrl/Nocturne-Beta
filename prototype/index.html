THE NOCTURNE JOURNAL

There is a kind of attention that arrives only when the room grows quiet. The work does not ask to be hurried. It asks for a surface, a little light, and the patience to begin again.

We keep the useful things close: a page worth returning to, a note not yet finished, a thought that might become something. The rest can wait in another room.
